\noindent\textbf{Status: development evidence only.} This report documents a training-only
pilot and must not be presented as the completed confirmatory LLM extension.

\section{Design}
The pilot selects three training worlds, one per evidence verdict, and four provenance conditions
per world: a single origin, four independent observations, a shared dataset, and ambiguous
provenance. Each case is tested with ordinary instructions, a dependence reminder, and the same
reminder plus EchoGraph's fallible proposed partition. Every arm receives the same four
gold-relevant documents. The output schema asks for an answer, evidence verdict, three-class
probabilities, and citations; the ordinary arm does not request source-family judgments.
The detector was fitted on the existing training split, which includes these worlds.

\section{Structured-output results}
Table~\ref{tab:pilot} reports only schema-valid outputs, with explicit denominators. Brier score
is the unhalved sum of squared errors over the three evidence-verdict probabilities.
It is not a measure of factual-truth calibration or confidence warranted by independent evidence.
The machine-readable logs and accompanying Markdown report retain all failed and invalid calls.
Requested working models are \texttt{qwen/qwen3.8-27b} through Groq and
\texttt{nvidia/nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b:free} through OpenRouter. Returned identifiers and
timestamps are recorded in the logs; hosted aliases are not immutable weight snapshots.
Gemini 3.8 Flash encountered service-availability errors; a documented alternative requests
Gemini 3.7 Flash in a separate run. Neither is pooled with the other.

\section{What this pilot cannot establish}
Correctly identifying a directly stated result does not demonstrate source-family reasoning.
A syntactically valid response may contain a placeholder, unsupported provenance claim, or
poor explanation. Such problems require additional output-quality checks and human review.
Only three worlds are present, and repeated arms are paired observations rather than independent
samples. No inferential significance tests, real-source generalization claims, or mitigation
effectiveness claims follow from this pilot.

\section{Required follow-up}
Separate provenance diagnostics and blinded human evaluation of corroboration assertions are
needed. A confirmatory study must specify fresh held-out material, additional grouping and
oracle controls, the number of worlds needed to detect a meaningful effect, and missing-output
handling before collection. Human annotations and the prepared real-source pilot remain pending.
An additional training-only audit found 577 of 2,250 synthetic lineage edges with reversed
publication chronology. A small development sample now repairs timestamps, but a complete
corrected benchmark and fresh evaluation are needed before submission.
