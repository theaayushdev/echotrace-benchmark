# Instructions for the independent reviewer

Thank you for reviewing these research examples. No AI expertise is required. This is a pilot
review of synthetic documents, not a test of your ability. You may stop or skip any example.

1. Open STARTER_PACKET.md. Read the documents and the answer for each example.
2. Open STARTER_REVIEWS.csv in a spreadsheet. The example IDs are already filled in.
3. Use a pseudonym such as reviewer_02 in reviewer_id. Enter your own judgments independently;
   do not ask an AI to fill them in or discuss judgments with the author before saving them.
4. In answer_substantive, enter yes if it actually answers the question, no for a placeholder.
5. In verdict_supported_by_text, enter yes/no/unclear: does the evidence support the answer's
   supported/contradicted/insufficient verdict? You are not verifying real-world truth.
6. In asserts_independent_corroboration, enter yes/no/unclear: does the answer say there are
   independent confirmations or separate observations? Merely saying several reports agree
   is not by itself an assertion of independence.
7. In provenance_assertion_warranted, enter yes/no/unclear/not_applicable. If independence is
   asserted, do the documents establish it? If no such assertion is made, use not_applicable.
8. In uncertainty_appropriate, enter yes/no/unclear/not_applicable. Does a provenance assertion
   respect what is unknown? Use not_applicable when the answer makes no provenance judgment.
9. Give a short rationale and a brief evidence_span quoting the relevant words. You may use
   unclear when the documents do not let you decide. Unknown provenance is not proof of copying.
10. Save as CSV and return the file to the author. Share no personal information beyond your
    chosen reviewer ID. Tell the author if you do not consent to releasing your anonymized labels.

Model identities, experiment conditions, and gold labels are intentionally withheld. The text
itself may reveal clues; perfect blinding is not assumed. The selected sample is balanced across
experimental groups for inspection and cannot estimate general error prevalence. Your participation
does not automatically imply authorship. Any institutional review requirements should be checked
by the author before using the judgments in a publication.
